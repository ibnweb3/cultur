CULTUR

New name. Familiar cultural footprint.

Cultur uses Qloo's cross-domain taste graph to surface possible connections between crypto creators and prior projects, with cited evidence and onchain corroboration. Taste similarity is a lead, never proof of identity or fraud.

Privacy. No accounts. No Twitter/X scraping. Analysis uses submitted public identifiers, cited public records, public onchain facts, and Qloo's aggregated cultural intelligence. Public handles and wallet addresses can still be personal data; submitted inputs and generated dossiers are kept for 7 days and then expire.

Why it matters


Americans reported $7.2 billion in cryptocurrency investment-fraud losses in 2025, according to the FBI's IC3 (2025 Internet Crime Report). That figure covers U.S. reports and is not attributable to creator rebrands. Cultur is due-diligence support for fans and creators, not a verdict on anyone.
