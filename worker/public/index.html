How it works

01

Check the audience.

Describe your project or enter references directly. Review the matched names before using a direction, especially when we suggested them.

02

Compare the choices.

Explore up to three palettes, font pairs and spacing systems. Each direction shows which choices came from audience data and which use defaults.

03

Use it in your build.

Copy the design brief to your coding agent or download the CSS. You can also check references and audit code against your chosen direction.

A starting point, with the sources to back it up.

Qloo provides taste relationships and descriptors, not colors or fonts. Our design mapping is a hypothesis, not a prediction of what every person will prefer. Every choice shows its source; choices without enough evidence are labeled as defaults.

Text and interface contrast pairs are checked. The completed interface still needs its own accessibility review. No language model writes the design tokens. Rule version: qv-rules-1.0.
