What the data can tell you

Qloo provides taste relationships and descriptors, not colors or fonts. Our design mapping is a hypothesis, not a prediction of what every person will prefer. Every choice shows its source; choices without enough evidence are labeled as defaults.

Text and interface contrast pairs are checked. The completed interface still needs its own accessibility review.
